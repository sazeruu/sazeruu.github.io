The problem

One resume for every job gets ignored.

Recruiters and applicant systems look for the words in the job post. A generic file rarely has them in the right place.

A normal resume
  • The same file goes to every company
  • You do not know if you actually fit the job
  • The email is generic, or missing
  • What the job asked for is easy to miss
With Sazeru
  • Matching skills moved to the top for each job
  • A fit score, with the skills you are missing
  • An email written for that role and company
  • Only your own facts, nothing invented
How it works

From blank page to sent in three steps.

Step 1

Build it once

Add your details. The strength gauge shows what is missing, so you know exactly what to add.

Step 2

Paste a job post

Get a fit score, plus the skills you have and the ones the job asks for that you lack.

Step 3

Send it

Download the tailored PDF, copy the email, send it from your own address and mark the job as applied.

Questions

Quick answers.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything is free while we launch. Paid plans, starting at ₹99, are planned for later.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Open it and start. Your resume is saved in your browser on this device.

Does it make up experience?

No. It only uses what you type. Always read the result before you send it.

Which job sites does it work with?

All of them. Copy the job post text from LinkedIn, Naukri, a company page or a message, and paste it in.

I am a fresher with no job experience.

Add internships, college projects and certificates as experience lines. Write what you did, one line each.

Can it apply for me automatically?

Not yet. Sazeru prepares everything and you send. AI writing and an approval-first auto-apply robot are planned.
