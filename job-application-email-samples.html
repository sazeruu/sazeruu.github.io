Job application email samples for freshers

Updated 2026-10-12 · Free guide by Sazeru Analytics

A short, clear email makes it easy for a recruiter to open your resume. Below are copy-and-edit samples for a fresher: applying to a posted job, applying with a referral, a cold email and a follow-up. Replace the words in [brackets].

Quick rules

  • Keep it to 100 to 150 words.
  • Put the role name in the subject line.
  • Attach your resume as a PDF with a clear file name.
  • Mention 2 or 3 skills that match the job.
  • Send it to a named person if you can find one. Check names and spelling twice.

Sample 1: Applying to a posted job

Subject: Application for Junior Data Analyst - [Your Name]

Dear [Hiring Manager's name],

I am writing to apply for the Junior Data Analyst role posted on [website].
I recently completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and have worked with
SQL, Excel and Power BI during my internship at [Company], where I built a
dashboard used by store managers.

I have attached my resume for your review. I would be glad to discuss how
I can contribute to your team.

Thank you for your time.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone] | [Email]

Sample 2: Applying with a referral

Subject: Referred by [Name] - Junior Data Analyst Application

Dear [Name],

[Referrer's name] suggested that I reach out to you about the Junior Data
Analyst opening at [Company]. I have hands-on experience with SQL, Excel and
Power BI from my internship and college projects.

My resume is attached. I would appreciate the chance to speak with you.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[Phone] | [Email]

Sample 3: Cold email when no job is posted

Subject: Fresher with SQL and Power BI skills - interested in data roles

Dear [Name],

I am a recent B.Tech graduate and I am interested in data analyst
opportunities at [Company]. I have built a sales dashboard in Power BI and
cleaned business data in Excel and SQL.

If you have any suitable openings now or in the future, I would be grateful
if you could consider my resume, which is attached.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
[Phone] | [Email]

Sample 4: Follow-up after one week

Subject: Following up: Junior Data Analyst application - [Your Name]

Dear [Name],

I applied for the Junior Data Analyst role on [date] and wanted to follow up
politely. I remain very interested in the position and would be happy to
share any extra information you need.

Thank you for your time.

Regards,
[Your Name]

Do not: send the same email to many companies without changing the company and role, write in all capital letters, or forget to attach the resume.

Subject line ideas

  • Application for [Role] - [Your Name]
  • [Role] Application - [Skill 1] and [Skill 2]
  • Referred by [Name] - [Role]

Frequently asked questions

Should I write a cover letter as well?

A short email like these usually works as the cover letter. Only add a separate letter if the job post asks for it.

When should I follow up?

Wait about a week, send one polite follow-up, and then move on to other applications.
