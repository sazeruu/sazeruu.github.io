Resume format for freshers in India

Updated 2026-10-12 · Free guide by Sazeru Analytics

If you are a fresher, recruiters spend only a few seconds on your resume. A clean, one-page format that puts your strongest points first helps them see you quickly. Here is a simple format that works for most private-sector jobs and internships in India.

The best section order for a fresher

  1. Name and contact details: full name, phone, professional email, city, LinkedIn or GitHub link.
  2. Summary: 2 to 3 lines on who you are and the role you want.
  3. Education: put this near the top because you have little work experience.
  4. Skills: tools and skills that match the job.
  5. Projects: 2 to 3 projects, with what you built and what happened.
  6. Internships or experience: if you have any, even short ones.
  7. Certifications and achievements: only the relevant ones.

A simple fresher resume template

YOUR NAME
Phone | email@example.com | City, State | linkedin.com/in/yourname

SUMMARY
B.Tech graduate in Computer Science with hands-on experience in
SQL and Excel. Looking for a junior data analyst role.

EDUCATION
B.Tech, Computer Science - Your College, City | 2022 - 2026 | CGPA 8.2
Class XII - Your College | 2022 | 92%

SKILLS
SQL, Excel, Python, Power BI, Communication

PROJECTS
Sales Dashboard - Built a Power BI dashboard on 10,000 rows of sales
data. Helped identify the top 3 products by revenue.

INTERNSHIP
Data Analyst Intern - Company Name | Jan 2026 - Jun 2026
- Cleaned weekly sales data using Excel and SQL
- Presented findings to the team every Friday

What to leave out

  • Date of birth, religion, father's name and full home address. Most companies do not need these. Your city is enough.
  • A photo, unless the company asks for one.
  • Vague lines such as "hard-working and passionate". Show it with a project or result instead.
  • Skills you cannot talk about. If you list Python, be ready to answer a basic Python question.

Tips that make a fresher resume stronger

  • Keep it to one page.
  • Use numbers where you can: "10,000 rows", "weekly reports", "team of 4".
  • Start each bullet with an action word: built, cleaned, designed, presented.
  • Change a few lines for each job, so your skills match the words in that job post.
  • Save it as a PDF named like Yourname_Role_Resume.pdf.

Frequently asked questions

Should a fresher's resume be one page?

Yes. With little experience, one page is enough and is easier for a recruiter to read quickly.

Should I put education first or skills first?

Education first works well for freshers. If you have strong projects or internships, you can move them above education.

Do I need a career objective?

A short summary of 2 to 3 lines is better than a generic objective. Name the role and one or two strengths.

What if I have no experience?

Use projects, college work, volunteering, certifications and tools you have learned. Describe what you did and the result.
